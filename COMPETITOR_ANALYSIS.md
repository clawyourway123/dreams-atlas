## Why Each Competitor Matters

### **Recursion (High-Throughput Screening)**
- **Threat:** Massive scale, venture-backed, image-first
- **Defense:** DreaMS is spectra-first; complementary use case. Recursion uses HTS images (cells/tissues); we use chemical spectra (composition). Different modality, different customer.

### **Enveda (Natural Products)**
- **Threat:** Growing, well-funded, focused on bioactivity
- **Defense:** DreaMS is agnostic to bioactivity; it's a *discovery platform*. Enveda finds hits; DreaMS accelerates their search process.

### **Terray (Automated Chemistry)**
- **Threat:** Fully automated end-to-end synthesis
- **Defense:** Terray outputs chemistry; DreaMS *explores* existing chemistry. Complementary: Terray generates candidates; DreaMS helps select which ones to prioritize.
